Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your BizNova account and the personal information associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@biznova.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the email address registered to your BizNova account so we can locate it.
  3. Do not include your password or other sensitive information in the request.

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What happens next

After verification, we will delete your account and associated information, including saved business plans and logos, unless we are required to retain particular information by law or for legitimate security purposes. We will let you know if any information must be retained.

For details about the information BizNova collects and uses, see our Privacy Policy.
