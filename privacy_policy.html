Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

BizNova respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using our application.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password hash when you create an account.
  • Content you provide: business ideas, plans, logo prompts, uploaded text, and other inputs you submit to the app.
  • Usage data: basic technical data such as log entries, timestamps, and actions taken within the app.
  • Cookies/session data: we use session cookies to keep you signed in and improve security.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To provide app features such as business plan generation, logo generation, and PDF export.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • To improve the application and its user experience.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when necessary to operate the app, comply with the law, or protect our rights. For example, third-party service providers may process data on our behalf to deliver AI or hosting services.

4. Data storage and security

Your data may be stored in our database and application logs. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

6. Your choices

  • You may update or delete content you create inside the app where available.
  • You can stop using the service at any time.
  • If you want us to delete your account data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s privacy

BizNova is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the BizNova team at support@biznova.com.
